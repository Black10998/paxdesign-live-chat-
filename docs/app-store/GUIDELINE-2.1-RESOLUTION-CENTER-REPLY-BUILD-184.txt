Guideline 2.1(a) — Chat attachments
We reproduced the issue: the (+) menu in customer chat showed Camera, Photo Library, Voice message, and Location, but blocked those actions with a message that attachments were only available during human support.

This was incorrect product behavior. In version 2.1.1 (build 184) we fixed it so customer chat attachments work at all times — including while chatting with the AI assistant before a human agent joins. The options in the (+) menu now perform their intended action instead of showing a blocking message.

Guideline 3.2 — Public distribution
PAXDesign is a public customer app on the App Store. Any user can download the app, create a free account in-app, and use the customer portal (Home, Services, Portfolio, Chat, Account). No invitation, organization membership, or staff credentials are required.

Please sign in with the customer demo account in App Review Information to evaluate the public experience. The app opens directly to the customer portal.

Staff-specific tools exist only for accounts with server-side staff permission and are not part of the public App Store offering.
