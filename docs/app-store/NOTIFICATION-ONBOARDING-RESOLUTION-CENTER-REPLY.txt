Hello App Review Team,

Thank you for the report. We identified and fixed the issue where the notification permission screen could block access to the app.

What we fixed in Build 160:
- The "Enable Notifications" and "Not Now" buttons now respond immediately on the notification education sheet.
- "Not Now" dismisses the sheet and continues into the app without requiring notification permission.
- "Enable Notifications" presents the standard iOS permission dialog; the app continues whether the user allows or denies notifications.
- Notification permission is optional throughout onboarding.
- We removed UI issues that could prevent taps (sheet binding, dismiss timing, and layout).

How to verify:
1. Delete the app completely from the device (fresh install).
2. Sign in with the demo account provided in App Store Connect.
3. On the notification screen, tap "Not Now" — the app should open immediately.
4. Repeat with a fresh install and tap "Enable Notifications", then choose Allow or Don't Allow — the app should open in both cases.

Build 160 is available in TestFlight and submitted for review.

Thank you.
